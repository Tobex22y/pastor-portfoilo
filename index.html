Biography

Transforming Lives Across Corporate Finance, Ministry & Youth Development

BELOVED Akinkunmi Abimbola is a seasoned banker, transformational leader, financial expert, author, and passionate youth mentor whose influence spans corporate finance, ministry, leadership development, and community service.

He currently serves as the Executive Director of Asteric Asset Management, a Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)-approved financial institution. In this role, he contributes to the strategic direction, operational growth, investment management, and overall development of the organisation, drawing on his extensive experience in banking, finance, leadership, and customer relationship management.

Before joining Asteric Asset Management, Akinkunmi served at PremiumTrust Bank, where he oversaw strategic operations across multiple high-value branches in Ogun State. With a strong record of professional excellence, he consistently demonstrated the capacity to drive business growth, optimise organisational performance, strengthen customer relationships, and lead high-impact teams.

Prior to his tenure at PremiumTrust Bank, he served as the Zonal Head, Southwest 2, at Sterling Bank Plc, where he supervised banking operations across Ondo and Ekiti States. Under his leadership, the zone recorded significant growth, achieved double-digit market share expansion in priority sectors, and consistently surpassed revenue targets for three consecutive years.

Beyond his professional career, Akinkunmi is a devoted follower of Christ and a passionate teacher of the Word of God. He is the pioneer Pastor of the Youth Church of The Gospel Faith Mission International (GOFAMINT), Pentecost Arena, Ikere-Ekiti, an initiative established to raise leaders who are spiritually grounded, purpose-driven, and socially relevant. Weekly, he mentors more than 1,000 young people, including students, corps members, entrepreneurs, and young professionals.

Fondly known as "Beloved," he is widely recognised for his apostolic calling, prophetic grace, passion for human development, financial expertise, and unwavering commitment to raising a generation of leaders who are earthly relevant and heavenly conscious. He is happily married to Glorious Abimbola, and their union is blessed with children who actively serve God with their time, gifts, and talents.
