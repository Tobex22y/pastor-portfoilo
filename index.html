Biography

A Life Anchored in God's Word & Marketplace Excellence

Pastor Beloved Opeyemi Abimbola is an ordained minister of the Gospel, seasoned corporate banking executive, author, and conference speaker based in Lagos, Nigeria.

For over two decades, Pastor Beloved has walked the dual corridors of spiritual pulpit ministry and boardroom executive finance. He has served as a trusted advisor to banking institutions, corporations, and emerging entrepreneurs, teaching the principles of righteous stewardship, financial integrity, and kingdom governance.

Through his apostolic teachings, executive seminars, and his bestselling book More Than Gold, he helps believers bridge the gap between spiritual devotion on Sunday and high-integrity productivity on Monday morning.

“
Gold can purchase clocks, but cannot buy time. It can build a house, but never a home. When divine purpose takes the throne, you discover true wealth that money cannot buy and death cannot steal.
— Pastor Beloved O. Abimbola
